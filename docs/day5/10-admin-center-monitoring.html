Admin Center Monitoring

The Fabric Admin Center provides capacity-level monitoring for administrators. It shows how all workloads across tenants and workspaces contribute to overall resource usage.

Key Admin Center monitoring features:

  • Capacity health and status indicators
  • CU consumption graphs over time
  • Alerts and warnings for high pressure or throttling
  • Breakdowns by workload type

Admin Center monitoring is essential for capacity planning and for understanding when to scale, redistribute, or optimize workloads.
