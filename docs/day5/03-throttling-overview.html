Throttling Overview

Throttling in Microsoft Fabric is a protective mechanism that temporarily slows down or limits workloads when demand exceeds available resources. It is designed to maintain overall system stability and fairness across tenants and workloads.

Key points about throttling:

  • It is not a failure, but a safeguard.
  • It occurs when capacity pressure crosses certain thresholds.
  • Different workloads experience throttling in different ways.
  • Monitoring helps you see when and where throttling happens.

Understanding throttling behavior is essential for designing reliable solutions that perform predictably under varying load.
