Capacity Metrics

Capacity metrics describe how much of a Fabric capacity is being used and how that usage changes over time. They are central to understanding when the system is under normal load versus high pressure.

Important capacity metrics include:

  • Peak CU usage during specific intervals
  • Average CU usage across days or weeks
  • Distribution of CU usage by workload type
  • Frequency and duration of high-pressure periods

Tracking these metrics helps you decide whether to scale capacity, reschedule jobs, or optimize workloads to fit within existing limits.
