Capacity Pressure Signals

Capacity pressure signals indicate that workloads are approaching or exceeding the available resources in a Fabric capacity. These signals help administrators and developers understand when the system is under stress.

Common capacity pressure indicators include:

  • Spikes in Capacity Units (CU) consumption
  • Consistently high average CU usage over time
  • Alerts or warnings in the Fabric Admin Center
  • Slow or delayed execution of workloads

By watching these signals, you can proactively adjust workloads, reschedule heavy jobs, or scale capacity before throttling becomes frequent.
