Power BI Throttling

Power BI workloads in Fabric, such as dataset refreshes and report queries, can be throttled when they consume too many resources or overlap heavily with other workloads.

Common Power BI throttling scenarios:

  • Dataset refreshes delayed or slowed under capacity pressure
  • Report queries responding more slowly during peak times
  • Limits on concurrent refresh operations

To manage Power BI throttling:

  • Schedule refreshes during off-peak hours
  • Optimize data models and measures
  • Monitor capacity usage around refresh windows
