Monitoring Overview in Microsoft Fabric

Monitoring in Microsoft Fabric is the ongoing process of observing workload behavior, resource usage, and system health across pipelines, notebooks, SQL endpoints, and Power BI. It provides the visibility needed to understand how the platform responds under different levels of demand.

Effective monitoring helps you:

  • Detect performance issues early
  • Understand capacity usage patterns
  • Identify workloads that cause pressure on the system
  • Prepare for and interpret throttling events

Monitoring and throttling are closely related: monitoring tells you what is happening, throttling shows how Fabric protects itself and your workloads when limits are reached.
