Throttling Triggers

Throttling is triggered when workloads collectively push a Fabric capacity beyond safe operating limits. These triggers are based on resource usage, concurrency, and workload patterns.

Common throttling triggers include:

  • High concurrency of pipelines or notebook jobs
  • Long-running or resource-intensive notebook sessions
  • Heavy SQL query workloads on a warehouse
  • Frequent or overlapping Power BI dataset refreshes

Recognizing these triggers helps you design schedules, optimize queries, and distribute workloads to reduce the likelihood of throttling.
